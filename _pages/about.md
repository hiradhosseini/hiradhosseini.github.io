Howdy! I'm currently a first-year medical student at the [Keck School of Medicine of USC](https://keck.usc.edu/). I'm passionate about utilizing computational tools to better understand medical phenomena. 

## Research
I am currently contributing to several projects dedicated to elucidating the pathophysiology of congential stationary night blindness (CSNB) at the [Nagiel Laboratory](https://www.nagiellab.org/) in Children's Hospital Los Angeles. 

Previously, I aided in the development of a RNA-protein complex visualization tool at the [Rohs Lab](https://www.rohslab.org/){:target="_blank"} in the Quantitative & Computational Biology department. You can check out our tool RNAProDB [here](https://rohslab.usc.edu/rnaprodb/){:target="_blank"}!

I was also a Data Science Research Assistant involved in the [Predicting What We Breathe](https://airquality.lacity.org/){:target="_blank"} project funded by a NASA grant for Advanced Information Systems Technology. My work focused on leveraging city-wide low-cost sensor data and state of the art machine learning and artificial intelligence approaches to construct the first [Digital Twin City](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2096232021000238){:target="_blank"} of Los Angeles.
